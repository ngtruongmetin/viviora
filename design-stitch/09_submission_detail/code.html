Dune

Frank Herbert

star star star star star

NHẬN XÉT CỦA HỌC SINH

I wasn't sure if I would like Dune because I thought it would be just another slow sci-fi book, but the political intrigue completely hooked me. The way Herbert builds the world of Arrakis makes it feel like a real place that you can almost taste the sand.

Paul's journey is fascinating, though sometimes he feels a bit too perfect. What I really loved was the ecology aspect and how water is treated as the most precious currency. It made me think a lot about our own world's resources.

The vocabulary was challenging in parts, especially keeping track of all the different factions like the Bene Gesserit and the Spacing Guild, but the glossary helped. Definitely a 5-star read for me, though I needed a break after finishing it because it's so dense.
